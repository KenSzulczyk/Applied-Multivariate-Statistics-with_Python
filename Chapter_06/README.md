## 📈 Multiple Regression Analysis

We extend simple regression by including multiple explanatory variables simultaneously.

This allows us to:

* isolate independent effects,
* reduce omitted variable bias,
* and improve explanatory power.

Using Python, we estimate:

* regression coefficients,
* standard errors,
* t-tests,
* and model fit statistics.

The chapter emphasizes interpretation and economic meaning rather than simply estimating equations.

## 📉 Regression Diagnostics

Statistical models are only useful when their assumptions are reasonably satisfied.

We examine important diagnostic issues including:

🔍 Multicollinearity

Occurs when explanatory variables are highly correlated.

This can:

* inflate standard errors,
* weaken statistical significance,
* and make interpretation difficult.

We introduce correlation matrices.

## 🔄 Autocorrelation

Time series data often exhibit correlation across time periods.

This violates standard regression assumptions and may lead to unreliable inference.

We examine:

* residual plots,
* Durbin–Watson statistics,
* and the consequences of serial correlation.

🔮 Forecasting Models

Forecasting allows businesses and researchers to make predictions about future outcomes.
